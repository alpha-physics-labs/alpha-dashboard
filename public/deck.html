01 · Where we started

We were asked to find a lighter body armour material.

Hacking for Defense. A real problem, a real sponsor, a clear ask. So we went looking.

We searched literature. We searched databases. For most candidate compositions, there was simply nothing to find.

02 · What we learned

We were solving the wrong problem.

Nobody is short of candidate materials. An engineer can list forty in an afternoon. What they cannot do is find out which are worth making.

The original ask

Find one material

Answers one question, once. Then the next programme asks again.

What we built instead

Predict any material

Answers the question for every candidate, in every programme, in one second.

03 · How it works

Physics answers first. The model only corrects it.

INPUT

A formula

Becomes 132 measurable physical quantities of its elements.

PRIOR

A physical law

Stiffness rises with bond strength and packing. The law answers first.

LEARNING

A correction

The network learns only the gap, and is penalised for physically impossible behaviour.

OUTPUT

Exact equations

Nine of twelve properties come from classical physics, never guessed.

This is why it works on twenty samples, which is what a real customer test campaign actually produces.
