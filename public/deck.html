01 · Where we started

We were asked to find a lighter body armour material.

Hacking for Defense. A real sponsor, a real problem, a clear ask. So we went looking.

We searched literature. We searched databases. For most candidate compositions, there was nothing to find.

02 · What we learned

We were solving the wrong problem.

Nobody is short of candidate materials. An engineer lists forty in an afternoon. What they cannot do is find out which are worth making.

The original ask

Find one material

Answers one question, once. Then the next programme asks again.

What we built instead

Predict any material

Answers it for every candidate, in every programme, in one second.

05 · How it works

Physics answers first. The model only corrects it.

INPUT

A formula

Becomes 132 measurable physical quantities of its elements.

PRIOR

A physical law

Stiffness rises with bond strength and packing. The law answers first.

LEARNING

A correction

The network learns only the gap, and is penalised for impossible behaviour.

OUTPUT

Exact equations

Nine of twelve properties come from classical physics, never guessed.

This is why it works on twenty samples — what a real test campaign produces.
